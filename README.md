Platinum Road — a complete GTA VI trophy guide and tracker, built offline in the browser. All 55 official PlayStation 5 trophies (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze) with a real guide for each, written in our own words and citing its sources. A six-phase roadmap to the platinum, a dedicated missables view, filters by grade and progress, a spoiler setting that masks hidden trophies until you reveal or earn them, and progress saved in the browser with export and import.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
